# 3D printer

A Bambu Lab P2S with an AMS 2 Pro, told October 18, 2026.

## Keeping filament dry

- The AMS 2 Pro dries with a heater, so desiccant in it wants to be heat-stable:
  molecular sieve or activated alumina. Bambu recommends molecular sieve over plain
  silica gel for the heated unit, because silica gel gives moisture back as it warms.
- The desiccant holders take the common 50 g sachet, roughly 65 x 45 x 15 mm. Bambu's
  own refill is a 6-pack and 2 to 3 packs go in at a time. Larger packs (100 g and up)
  force the holder open and are the wrong shape for it.
- He also stores filament in ziplock bags, and wants one kind of pack for both.
- Packs of this sort are rechargeable: activated alumina dries out again at about
  250 F in the oven, molecular sieve likewise but slower.
