# 3D printer

A Bambu Lab P2S with an AMS 2 Pro, told October 18, 2026.

## Keeping filament dry

- The AMS 2 Pro dries with a heater, so desiccant in it wants to be heat-stable:
  molecular sieve or activated alumina. Bambu recommends molecular sieve over plain
  silica gel for the heated unit, because silica gel gives moisture back as it warms.
- The desiccant holders take the common 50 g sachet, roughly 65 x 45 x 15 mm. Bambu's
  own refill is a 6-pack and 2 to 3 packs go in at a time. Larger packs (100 g and up)
  force the holder open and are the wrong shape for it.
- He also stores filament in ziplock bags, and wants one kind of pack for both.
- Packs of this sort are rechargeable: activated alumina dries out again at about
  250 F in the oven, molecular sieve likewise but slower.
## Using the desiccant packs

Answered October 19, 2026, for the ADSORBCO 50 g activated alumina packs.

- 2 to 3 packs go in the AMS 2 Pro desiccant bay at a time, which is what Bambu
  says for its own 6-pack refill. One pack per ziplock bag of filament. The
  8-pack covers three in the AMS plus five bags.
- No fixed swap interval. Activated alumina is white with no colour-change dye,
  unlike Bambu silica gel and molecular sieve whose orange beads turn purple, so
  saturation cannot be seen: go by the relative humidity the AMS 2 Pro reports in
  Handy. Under about 15 percent is comfortable for PLA and PETG; nylon and PVA
  want single digits. When the reading climbs past that and stays there, the
  packs are spent.
- The AMS 2 Pro drying cycle revives the packs in place, since it dries whatever
  is in the chamber: 65 C, its maximum, for 4 to 12 hours. A tested comparison on
  the Bambu forum found 12 hours at 65 C as dry as an oven at 120 C. It works
  best with the unit empty of filament.
- A full reset of alumina wants more heat than the AMS can give: about 250 F for
  2 to 3 hours in the oven.
- Three to six months is the usual life before a pack stops recovering and wants
  replacing. Bambu gives no interval of its own, only dry or replace when spent.
