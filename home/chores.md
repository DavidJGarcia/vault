# Chores

## The litter robot

The cat box is a Litter-Robot, a self-cleaning box that rakes waste into a drawer
underneath. Emptying it means pulling that drawer — not changing the litter, and
not the kids' trash-and-litter turn below.

**Never more than 5 days between emptyings**, his standing word of October 9,
2026: "Don't ever let me go more than 5 days without emptying it." A reminder
stands for it, every 5 days at 6 p.m., and it is moved forward each time he says
he emptied it. The rule itself is in `instructions.md`.

| Emptied | By | Since the last |
|---|---|---|
| Friday, October 9, 2026, 10:15 p.m. | David himself | — |
| Wednesday, October 14, 2026, 7:27 a.m. | David himself | 4 days 9 hours |
| Sunday, October 18, 2026, 9:49 a.m. | David himself | 4 days 2 hours |
| Friday, October 23, 2026, 9:41 p.m. | David himself | 5 days 12 hours — over |
| Wednesday, October 28, 2026, evening | David himself | about 4 days 22 hours |
| Tuesday, November 4, 2026, night | David himself | about 7 days — over |
| Monday, November 9, 2026, evening | David himself | about 5 days — at the limit |

Next due: Saturday, November 14, 2026, 6 p.m.

Each emptying is now a record of its own kind, `litter_emptying`, born on the
October 14 one.

**The rule was passed for the first time on October 23, 2026**, by about twelve
hours. The reminder fired that day at 6 p.m. and he emptied it at 9:41 p.m.;
because he gave no time beyond "today", the emptying is filed at the moment he
spoke, so the real gap may have been shorter. The three earlier emptyings all
held, at four days and a few hours each.

That break is what brought the October 24, 2026 amendment in `instructions.md`:
the reminder now falls on 6 p.m. of the last day whose 6 p.m. is still inside
five days — the emptying day plus four when he emptied it before 6 p.m., plus
five when at or after. The October 28 emptying was an evening one and held, at
about 4 days 22 hours; it was reported two days later, on Friday October 30, as
"I emptied the litter box Wednesday evening", with no hour, so it is filed at
8 p.m.

**The second break, and the longest gap so far, was November 4, 2026**: about
seven days after the October 28 emptying, two days over the maximum. The
reminder fired on Monday November 2 at 6 p.m. and he pulled the drawer on
Tuesday night, reporting it the next morning as "I took out the Litter Robot
trash last night". So the amended timing is not enough on its own — the reminder
landed on the right evening and the drawer waited anyway. The October 30, 2026
follow-up rule is what covers that: the morning briefing names the overdue
drawer every morning until an emptying is filed.

**November 9, 2026 held, on the evening it was due.** He reported it two mornings
later — "I emptied the litter box on the day it was due, Monday" — after the
briefing had carried the past-due row on the 10th and the 11th, which is the
follow-up rule working as intended. No hour was given, so it is filed at 8 p.m.,
making the gap exactly five days: the rule held, at its edge.

## Trash and kitty litter

Taking out the trash and changing the kitty litter go together, and the kids take
turns at the pair.

| Date | Whose turn |
|---|---|
| Saturday, September 26, 2026 | David (his son) |
| Monday, September 28, 2026 | Derek |
| Thursday, October 15, 2026 | Derek — his second turn |
| not yet taken | Abby — still owed, as of October 15, 2026 |

He tracks this out loud: "David is doing the trash and kitty litter today"
(September 26), "Derek's taking out trash and kitty litter but Abby still needs to
take her turn" (September 28), "Derek is doing trash and kitty litter today"
(October 15).

Changing the litter is not emptying the Litter-Robot drawer above, and a turn here
never moves that reminder. He also says "took out the Litter Robot trash" for
pulling the drawer, which is the chore above and not one of these turns.

**Abby's turn is not on any list.** A to-do, "Get Abby to do trash and litter", was
filed on September 28, 2026 and is no longer in the records, so he took it off
`/notes/todo` himself. Corrected here by the weekly pass of October 4, 2026, which
found this document claiming a to-do that does not exist. Nothing now tracks the
owed turn, and Derek has now taken two of the three turns on record.

Seventeen days passed between the September 28 turn and the October 15 one with
nothing said about these chores, so the record of turns is what he says out loud
rather than a schedule.
