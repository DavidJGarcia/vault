# Refrigerator

Kept because the coil question has come up twice (September 18 and September 19, 2026).

## Cleaning the condenser coils

1. Unplug the refrigerator.
2. Open the access: snap off the grille at the bottom front, or pull the
   cardboard panel off the back. Newer fridges are usually bottom-front.
3. Vacuum the coils with the crevice tool, then work a coil brush between the
   fins gently — the fins bend easily.
4. Vacuum up what the brush loosened, including the floor underneath.
5. Refit the grille or panel and plug it back in.

Twice a year is enough; every three or four months with pets shedding.

Sources: searspartsdirect.com, producthelp.whirlpool.com, basc.pnnl.gov.
