# nail_trim

Nails cut at home - his own, a kid's, or the cat's claws. Born Sunday,
October 18, 2026 from "I also trimmed my child's and my own fingernails and
toenails," the second trim of that morning: "I trimmed the cat" came a few
seconds earlier and is the first row of this kind.

props:  who (list: David, a kid's name, cat) · nails (list: fingernails,
        toenails, claws) · by (string: who did the cutting) · next_due
        (YYYY-MM-DD, optional: the soonest usual interval for what was cut)
enrich: first person is David himself, and he is the one cutting unless he says
        otherwise. "my child" with no name stays unnamed - he has three kids,
        David, Derek and Abby (`home/chores.md`), and guessing which would put a
        wrong name in the row. "trimmed the cat" means claws; fur is said as
        shaved or trimmed his fur. Intervals for next_due: fingernails every
        1-2 weeks, toenails every 6-8, a cat's claws every 2-4. Record the
        interval; set no reminder unless he asks for one.
ask:    "when did I last cut the cat's claws", "when did I last do the kids'
        nails"; weeks since, per subject.
notes:  he reports these after the fact and in a run - the cat and then the
        people in the same minute, the second note beginning with "also". So a
        note of this kind may be one of several, and each subject is its own
        row.
