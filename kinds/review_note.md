# review_note

Something he said into the ring for his annual reviews. Born October 27, 2026 from
"Upward review in transformation section, successful FDE pilot, reworking accrual approach,
and driving altmetric consulting approach" — the second review note, after
"Upward review: We're working on creating space for me to drive a process without the
outcome being predetermined" the same evening.

props:  half (self | upward) · section (the heading in the review view it was placed under) ·
        content (his words, tidied, never summarised into a competency phrase) ·
        occurred_on (YYYY-MM-DD, the day he said it) · items (list, when one note holds
        several distinct things) · named_section (true when he said the section himself)

enrich: place it by what it says when he does not name a section, and record that choice in
        section; a section he names outright is taken as said, and a new heading is added to
        the view rather than the note bent to fit an existing one. Keep acronyms as spoken —
        FDE, altmetric — they are his work's words, not to be expanded on a guess.

ask:    "what do I have for my self review"; everything under one half or one section, in
        date order, when the real form appears and the draft has to be written out.

notes:  the trigger is the opening of the note ("self review", "upward review"); the record
        exists so the review view can be rebuilt from the rows if it is ever lost. Every note
        also rewrites the view whole. A note that corrects or extends an earlier one edits
        that record and that bullet rather than adding a second.
