# standing_instruction

A standing word from David about how the worker should work from now on, spoken
as a capture rather than typed into instructions.md on GitHub. Born October 4,
2026 from "From now on when I weigh in, I want information about the trend not
the previous weigh-in." — the second of its sort. The first, September 21, 2026,
"From now on when you track food that I've eaten, also respond with total
calories and total protein for the day.", was untyped and was re-filed under this
kind when it was born.

props:  rule (string: what now stands, one line) · scope (what it governs: meal
        receipts, weigh-in receipts, a kind, a job) · applies_from (YYYY-MM-DD) ·
        stored_at (list: the files the rule was written into) · assumed (list)

enrich: name the scope narrowly — the receipts or the kind it changes, never
        "everything". Write the rule into instructions.md, newest at the end,
        under a heading for its subject, and into the kind's document too when it
        changes how that kind is filed or read back; list both in stored_at. When
        the rule changes a kind's shape, re-derive that kind's rows once and say
        how many. An instruction is not anchored to a moment, so lag changes
        nothing.

ask:    "what have I told you to do"; which rules stand, since when, and where
        each one is written down.

notes:  he says "From now on ..." and means it permanently. Every write to
        instructions.md gets its own wrist message, titled Instructions, so he
        always knows the standing word changed. The record is the account of his
        saying it; instructions.md is what is actually followed, because that
        file is appended to every job's prompt.
