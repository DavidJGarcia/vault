# status_check

A question about where something of his own stands, answered from his records and his
vault rather than from the world. Born Monday September 21, 2026 from "What do I need to
do to be ready for my trip?" — the second such ask after "What do you know about my dad's
situation?" on Friday September 18, which was re-derived into this shape when the kind
was born.

props:  asked (the question as read) · about_what (the thing asked after: a trip, a
        person, a list) · reading (what the question was taken to mean) · answer (the
        short answer he was given) · sources_used (list: kinds queried, record ids, vault
        paths) · facts (list: what the records actually said) · gaps (list: what is not
        stored, and so could not be answered) · vault (a file the answer was written to)
        · assumed (list)

enrich: query rather than scroll — `records --kind <k> --open`, `timer list`, and the one
        vault file for the thing, not a read of everything. Order the answer by what he
        should do or know first, not by when it was filed. Name the gaps: what is not
        stored is as much of the answer as what is. No web search — this is his own
        material.

ask:    "what did I ask after"; whether a question has been asked before and whether the
        answer changed; which questions keep turning up the same gap.

notes:  he asks in one short sentence and the thing he means is whatever is live that day
        — the trip on departure morning, dad the week he was admitted. A food question
        summed from his own rows is nutrition_check; a question answered from the world is
        question; this is the rest. The answer belongs on the wrist in his order of doing,
        which is usually not the order the rows were filed in.
