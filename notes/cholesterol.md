## Does cardio help?

**Yes for the ratio. Barely for ApoB.**

What aerobic training reliably does, from a 2023 multivariate meta-analysis of
57 randomised trials and 3,194 participants:

- the ratio cluster (TC/HDL-C, LDL-C/HDL-C, ApoB100/ApoA1) falls **0.201**
  (95% CI -0.291 to -0.111, p < 0.001);
- the antiatherogenic cluster (ApoA1, HDL2, HDL3) rises **0.047 mmol/L**
  (p = 0.010), about +2.3 mg/dL of ApoA1;
- HDL-C itself rises about **2.5 mg/dL** on average, and a little more in
  people with a BMI under 28, which he is at 22.9.

So TC/HDL 4.5 moving to roughly 4.1-4.2 over twelve weeks is a realistic
result, and HDL 41 is exactly the line it would come from.

What it does not do:

- **ApoB100 fell only 0.053 mmol/L and was not significant** (p = 0.087). It
  reached significance only in the better-quality trials, at -0.08.
- LDL-C falls about **7 mg/dL** across the exercise literature.
- Triglycerides are where aerobic training does its most dramatic work, and
  his are already 66. Nothing to win there.

ApoB 105 is the finding on this panel, and the November 13 read judged it
constitutional — a hepatic LDL-receptor set point or cholesterol
hyper-absorption — rather than dietary, in a man this lean (BMI 22.9) and this
insulin-sensitive (HOMA-IR 0.92) with triglycerides of 66. Diet moves it maybe
15 percent; a low-dose statin moves it 40. Cardio is not the lever for it.

## The dose, if he does it for the lipids

The threshold is real, not a rounding: below it the HDL effect does not appear
at all.

- **120 minutes a week minimum**, or about 900 kcal of weekly expenditure.
- **Sessions over 30 minutes.** Session duration was the single most important
  element of the prescription; shorter sessions produced no significant HDL
  rise however many of them there were.
- **Twelve weeks minimum** at moderate intensity or better (over 40% VO2max).
- **Better above 180 minutes a week at over 65% VO2max.**

## The better argument for cardio

It is not the lipid panel. Cardiorespiratory fitness and blood pressure both
lower ASCVD risk independent of cholesterol, and blood pressure is still the
one missing input in his own risk estimate — no cuff reading of his has ever
been recorded. The HRV list of November 9, 2026 led with the same gap: all 243
of his logged exercise sets since September 14, 2026 are resistance machines.

Three sessions of 40 minutes a week clears every threshold above, and the
lipid improvement would be the smallest of the things it bought.

## Caveats

These are group means from mixed populations. A lean, already-trained man
usually sits at the small end of the LDL response and the large end of the HDL
response. Nothing here is a diagnosis or a prescription.
