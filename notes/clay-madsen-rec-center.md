# Clay Madsen Recreation Center

Round Rock's city rec center, 1600 Gattis School Rd, Round Rock TX 78664,
512-218-3220. About a mile west of Cedar Ridge High School (2801 Gattis School
Rd), which is why it came up: a place for Derek to shower on Saturday,
October 31, 2026. Saturday hours 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.

## Getting in

Nothing can be arranged in advance. The city's online portal
(RoundRockRecreation.com, a WebTrac system) sells class and program
registrations, not walk-in day passes or youth ID cards, and a first-time
visitor under 18 needs a parent or guardian at the desk to sign the waiver.
So it is a walk-in, about five minutes at the front desk.

Bring photo ID and proof of residence (a driver's licence covers both), and the
child.

## Who has to sign up

Only the one who is going in. Signing a child's waiver happens at the front
desk and costs nothing — there is no pass to buy to stand in the lobby. An
adult pays only if he uses the facility himself.

- Derek alone, first visit: **$6** (a $1 youth walk-in plus the one-time $5 ID
  card). $1 a visit after that, with the card.
- David going in too: **$5** more, the adult visitor pass. The visitor pass is
  18 and over, so an adult cannot be put on a youth walk-in.
- Youth means 20 and under here. An individual youth membership is $17 a month
  or $85 a year, which only pays off past about 17 visits in a year.

## Age rules worth knowing

- Derek is 12 (13 on November 10, 2026) and may be in the building on his own
  during normal hours. The only accompaniment rule for the facility is **after
  9 p.m.**, when 12 and under needs a guardian 16 or older.
- The **pool** is stricter: 12 and under must be actively supervised by a parent
  or guardian at all times, unsupervised use starts at 13. A shower in the
  locker room is not the pool, but a swim on Saturday would mean David goes in
  and pays the $5.
- Under 18 needs a parent or guardian signature to start a membership, not only
  a walk-in waiver.
