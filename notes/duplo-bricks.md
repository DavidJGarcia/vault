# Duplo bricks in bulk

Asked November 12, 2026: plain standard Duplo bricks — 2x2, 2x4, 2x6, 2x8 —
shipped to Central Texas for a reasonable price. He first said hundreds, then
corrected it the same afternoon: **thousands**. That correction changes the
answer, because at thousands you stop buying bricks and start buying weight.

## The part numbers

| Size | BrickLink part | Weight | Lots for sale |
|------|----------------|--------|---------------|
| 2x2  | 3437 | ~6.5 g | 9,248 |
| 2x4  | 3011 | 12.2 g | 3,696 |
| 2x6  | 2300 | ~18 g  | 542 |
| 2x8  | 4199 | ~24 g  | few hundred |

Plain means those bare numbers. A letter suffix (3437pb077, 4199pb02) is a
printed or decorated brick and costs more.

## Buy by the pound, not by the part

Used Duplo sold by weight runs **$4.46 to $8.50 a pound** in the larger lots
(November 2026; the cheap end is the 10–15 lb lots, and 5 lb specialty lots go
as high as $13). At 6.5 g and 12.2 g a brick:

- 2x2 — about 70 bricks to the pound
- 2x4 — about 37 bricks to the pound
- half and half — about 48 bricks to the pound

So **2,000 bricks of the two common sizes is roughly 41 lb, or $185–350**, which
is **9 to 17 cents a brick** delivered. Buying the same 2,000 part by part on
BrickLink is 23 to 35 cents a brick once each store's shipping is counted — two
to three times the money. Add $30–60 for a 40 lb box to Texas where the listing
does not include shipping.

The catch: a by-weight lot is mixed. Call it half plain basic bricks and half
plates, windows, animals, vehicles and printed pieces. If all 2,000 must be
plain bricks, buy **80–100 lb**, or buy from a seller who sorts.

## The 2x6 and 2x8 are the real limit

They cannot be had in thousands at any price. The entire BrickLink market holds
542 lots of the 2x6 and a few hundred of the 2x8, and most Duplo lots are 10 to
50 pieces. **A couple of hundred of each is the practical ceiling**, and that
alone takes three or four stores. Plan the build around 2x2s and 2x4s and treat
the long bricks as a garnish.

## What one brick costs, used, part by part

Still useful for topping up a size. Ranges across colors and sellers,
November 2026:

- 2x2 — 5 to 15 cents
- 2x4 — 10 to 25 cents
- 2x6 — 32 to 50 cents
- 2x8 — 23 to 60 cents

Red, blue and yellow are the cheap colors; lime, orange and the grays run
higher. Used Acceptable is cheapest, Used Good is what most sellers list.

## How to buy it

1. **The thousands:** eBay, sold by weight. Sort by price per pound —
   joncraton.org/duplo-by-pound aggregates US listings that way and refreshes
   every 15 minutes. Prefer one big lot over three small ones; the per-pound
   price falls with size and you pay one shipment.
2. **Fixed-count lots** where you want known sizes: 100 Duplo 2x4 multicolor
   $18, 100 2x2 $12, free shipping (listed November 12, 2026) — 12 to 18 cents
   a brick, no sorting, no surprises.
3. **BrickLink** last, for the 2x6 and 2x8 only. Build a Wanted List with parts
   2300 and 4199, color "any", condition Used, filter stores to the United
   States, and prefer a store that covers both sizes — one shipping charge beats
   two.
