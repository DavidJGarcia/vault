# Duplo bricks in bulk

Asked November 12, 2026: a few hundred plain standard Duplo bricks — 2x2, 2x4,
2x6, 2x8 — shipped to Central Texas for a reasonable price.

## The part numbers

| Size | BrickLink part | Lots for sale |
|------|----------------|---------------|
| 2x2  | 3437 | 9,248 |
| 2x4  | 3011 | 3,696 |
| 2x6  | 2300 | 542 |
| 2x8  | 4199 | few hundred |

Plain means those bare numbers. A letter suffix (3437pb077, 4199pb02) is a
printed or decorated brick and costs more.

## What one brick costs, used

Ranges across colors and sellers, November 2026:

- 2x2 — 5 to 15 cents
- 2x4 — 10 to 25 cents
- 2x6 — 32 to 50 cents
- 2x8 — 23 to 60 cents

Red, blue and yellow are the cheap colors; lime, orange and the grays run
higher. Used Acceptable is cheapest, Used Good is what most sellers list.

## What 400 mixed bricks comes to

Say 150 of the 2x2, 150 of the 2x4, 60 of the 2x6, 40 of the 2x8:

- parts: $70 at the low end of the ranges, $115 at the high end
- shipping: about 8–9 lb of plastic, so $20–25 USPS Ground Advantage per store
- all in: roughly $90–140, or 23 to 35 cents a brick

## The catch on BrickLink

Supply is lopsided. There are 9,248 lots of the 2x2 and 542 of the 2x6, and
almost nobody keeps hundreds of 2x6 or 2x8 in one place — most Duplo lots are
10 to 50 pieces. So a four-size order is three or four stores, and each one
charges its own shipping. That is what makes BrickLink expensive for bulk
basics even when the per-brick prices look low.

## How to buy it there

1. Build a Wanted List with the four part numbers and the quantities, color
   "any", condition Used.
2. Filter stores to the United States (Easy Buy cannot deselect international
   sellers, so use the Wanted List's store match instead and sort by location).
3. Prefer a store that covers three or four of the sizes, even at a slightly
   higher per-brick price — one shipping charge beats two.

## Cheaper for the two common sizes

eBay bulk lots, free shipping, US sellers, as listed November 12, 2026:

- 100 Duplo 2x4, multicolor, $18
- 100 Duplo 2x2, multicolor, $12
- 250+ mixed Duplo bricks and blocks, $55

That is 12 to 22 cents a brick delivered, against 23 to 35 on BrickLink, and
no stacked shipping. The mixed lots do include some printed and specialty
pieces. BrickLink is still the better place for the 2x6 and 2x8, which bulk
lots rarely carry in quantity.
