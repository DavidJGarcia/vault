times and distances are estimates from Manor, not routing lookups. Asked for
October 31, 2026; corrected November 7, 2026, when Hooky Hutto turned up.

The Bullock is the only screen near Austin that can run the 1.43:1 frame, but it
programs documentaries plus selected features rather than every wide release.
There is no IMAX 70mm film projector anywhere in central Texas.

Recliners and dine-in: EVO Kyle, EVO Belterra, Hooky Hutto. Bowling and an
arcade as well: Kyle and Hutto.

## Hooky Hutto

Opened May 23, 2025, the third Hooky Entertainment location: 54,400 sq ft, eight
auditoriums and 878 seats, IMAX with Laser in Theatre 1 (207 seats), two Dolby
Atmos houses, bowling, an arcade, virtual darts and a scratch kitchen. It was
missing from the October 31 ranking and from both November 1 comparisons, which
is why EVO Kyle was recommended; Hutto is 28 mi closer and the better trip
unless the picture itself is the point, and nobody publishes its screen size.
