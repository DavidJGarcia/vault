# US 290 and FM 973, Manor

The intersection two miles from the house, and what is going up on each corner.
Written November 1, 2026 after he asked about it three times in ten minutes.
Over 100 acres of retail across the four corners; traffic counts near 70,000
vehicles a day, the highest Manor will have.

## The corners

- **Northwest - Manor Crossing.** 63 acres, The Retail Connection. H-E-B
  (13100 FM 973, opened October 2025), Home Depot (135,000 sq ft, May 2025),
  Chick-fil-A (11900 E US 290, April 2025). Phase II is 150,000 sq ft in four
  buildings on 18 acres, broke ground October 31, 2024, due 2026: T.J. Maxx,
  Burlington, Planet Fitness, Five Below, Rack Room Shoes, James Avery,
  Jersey Mike's, McAlister's, Mattress Firm, Tropical Smoothie, Tomlinson's
  Feed, Twin Liquors. Killer Burger and Airi Ramen are finishing out inside it.
- **Northeast, southwest, southeast - Manor Commons.** Greenview Development,
  leased by Edge Realty (Hutch Hutchings, Cole Brodhead). Golden Chick is open
  at 11801 Ring Drive; 11924 Ring Drive holds The Learning Experience daycare
  and Results Physiotherapy in a 13,144 sq ft building from 2024. Pads filling
  in: Manor Commons North Bldg 1, a 15,500 sq ft retail shell, $2.3M, April to
  October 2026, no tenant named; a 3,000 sq ft shell for Greenview Development
  973 LP, March to December 2026.
- **Manor Commons Phase III.** 47 greenfield acres on the northeast corner,
  $71M of stores, restaurants and hotel space. Approved 2026. About $105M to
  the Manor tax base and 670 jobs, with the city rebating up to $9M in sales
  and property tax over 15 years. Site work is estimated 18 to 24 months
  before vertical construction starts.

## Southeast of the intersection - Manor Town Center

The big one, and the dirt he saw moving on November 1, 2026. South side of
US 290 just east of FM 973, at Bois d'Arc Lane: 13301 E US 290, about 84 acres
(1,287 feet of highway frontage, 2,412 feet deep). Landowner and master
developer Shenandoah Development Group; Hunt Real Estate & Infrastructure is
the city's P3 partner for the civic piece. Marketed as Manor Town Square.

Financial close and notice to proceed October 2, 2026. Groundbreaking for
Phase One enabling infrastructure - Phase 1 roads including the US 290 access,
wet utilities, surface parking, a pedestrian spine, bike and greenway
connections - is Saturday November 14, 2026, 10 a.m. to noon, at 13301 E US 290.

What is planned to be there:

- **City Hall** with council chamber, public service counters and community
  meeting space, on civic parcel P1 facing US 290.
- **Manor's first public library**, authorized by voters in the November 2023
  bond election. City Hall and library together are about 65,000 sq ft in
  current concepts, 35,000 of it library and community space. Open mid-2028.
- **Gathering lawn**, flexible open space with canopy trees and event
  infrastructure for festivals, farmers markets and concerts. The civic campus
  is 8.4 of the 84 acres.
- **A movie theater.**
- **Several hotels.**
- **A medical district** of office buildings.
- **Mixed-use residential over retail**, plus townhomes.
- Restaurants, dining and office, in a zero-setback walkable format.

About 2.2 million sq ft at full buildout. Designs beyond the civic campus are
not final and there is no construction timeline for the commercial phases.

## Nearby, not at the corner

- TXM Manor Emergency Room, 12838 US 290: 24,200 sq ft microhospital, $15M,
  architect Corgan, six inpatient rooms, two operating suites, six-bed ER.
  Final steel March 2026, TDLR completion August 31, 2026, no final inspection
  recorded.
- LongHorn Steakhouse, 12609 E US 290: 6,000 sq ft, $3.4M, opened June 29, 2026.
- HTeaO, 12211 US 290, opened April 11, 2026.
- IHOP, 13320 E US 290: 2,400 sq ft finish-out of leased space, 2026.
- Hobby Lobby, 14019 E US 290: 55,000 sq ft at Old Kimbro Road, mid-December
  2026 to late July 2027.
- FM 1100 Retail Center, 13105 US 290: 14,020 sq ft retail shell with a
  convenience store, $1.2M, November 1, 2026 to August 31, 2027.
- TxDOT and CAMPO are studying widening FM 973 from US 290 north to Samsung
  Highway. Public meetings April and May 2026; nothing built.
