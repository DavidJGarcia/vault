# Depositing a check made out to one of the kids

Worked out October 24, 2026, when a check arrived for Derek (12). He banks with
Wells Fargo, so this is the Wells Fargo route.

## The short answer

A 12-year-old cannot cash a check anywhere on his own. Retail check cashing
(Walmart, check-cashing stores) starts at 16 to 18 and needs a government photo
ID. The route is a branch, in person, with the check deposited into an account
an adult owns or co-owns.

## Where

Pflugerville, TX 78660 · (512) 354-3417. Lobby Monday–Friday 9:00–5:00,

Next nearest is **Hutto** — 604 W Hwy 79, Hutto, TX 78634 · (512) 846-2816,
same hours. There is no Wells Fargo branch in Manor or Elgin.

## How it works

1. Take the child with you, and take proof of who he is and that he is yours —
   birth certificate is what branches ask for; a passport or school ID helps.
2. The child endorses the back of the check in his own hand:
   `Derek Garcia — minor`
3. You sign beneath it, naming the relationship: `by David Garcia, parent`.
4. It is deposited into your account. A teller can do it; this is not a mobile
   deposit — Wells Fargo wants a minor's check handled in the branch, and mobile
   deposit requires the depositor to own the account.

The exact endorsement wording is the teller's call and varies by branch, so let
them look at it before signing anything.

## What it costs

**Nothing.** Depositing a check into your own Wells Fargo account is free, at the
teller or at the ATM. Check-cashing fees are for non-customers.

If he wants Derek to have somewhere of his own for it: Way2Save Savings, opened
**at a branch only** for anyone under 18, and under 13 it must be a joint account
with an adult co-owner — so David on the account alongside him. $25 to open (his
money, not a fee), and the $5 monthly service fee is waived while the primary
owner is 24 or under. The standalone teen checking account (Clear Access
Banking) starts at 13, so Derek is a year short of that.
