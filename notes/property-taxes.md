## What the county's own records would say, and why they are not here

The exact figure — what he paid Manor ISD each year, to the cent — is on one page:
the Travis County Tax Office account search, which holds every statement and receipt back
through the years and breaks each bill out by taxing unit. It is public and needs no login.

It cannot be read by the agent. The search is a JavaScript application
(`travis.go2gov.net/cart/responsive/search.do`, and the appraisal district's own portal
`travis.prodigycad.com/property-search`), it posts rather than takes a URL, and the agent's
browser is for shopping only. Every figure below is therefore **derived**, not quoted.

## What is solid and what is not

Solid:

- **The Manor ISD rate every year**, from the district itself: 2020 **1.442672**, 2021 and
  2022 **1.3520**, 2023 **1.0861**, 2024 and 2025 **1.0814**, 2026 **1.0755**. The rate has
  fallen 26 percent since he moved in — the 26-cent cut of 2023 was state compression, not
  Manor ISD choosing to charge less.
- **The school homestead exemption every year**, from state law: 25,000 through 2021,
  40,000 in 2022, 100,000 in 2023 and 2024, 140,000 from 2025. It rose five and a half
  times in six years.
- **2021 appraised value 328,091**, and the total 2021 bill across all units, $8,889.

Not solid — this is where the roughly $20,500 could move by a couple of thousand:

- **The appraised value in 2020, 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025.** Only 2021 is on record. The
  others are read from the 2019 bill ($9,114 at a 3.04 percent combined rate implies about
  $300,000), the 10 percent homestead cap on how fast an appraisal can rise, and the fact
  that Austin-area values spiked in 2022 and fell back after.
- **Whether a homestead exemption was on the house for 2020.** Before 2022 the exemption
  belonged to whoever owned on January 1, so it turns on the seller's filing, not his.

## Why the bill has halved while the house has not

The two numbers moved against each other. Appraised value is up about 13 percent since
2020; the ISD tax is down about 45 percent, from roughly $4,100 to $2,260. The whole of
that is the rate falling and the exemption rising — state legislation in 2019, 2023 and
2025 — and none of it is Manor ISD. Which is the context for the November 3, 2026 bond:
see the `question` records of October 21, 2026, where 3.85 cents of new I&S works out to
about $202 a year across both properties.

## To make this exact

Three ways, any one of which replaces the whole table above:

1. The Travis County Tax Office account search, by hand, on TCAD 700631 — statements and
   receipts per year, each broken out by taxing unit.
2. His mortgage escrow statements, which name the disbursement to each unit.
3. Form 50-114 history or a tax certificate from the county (there is a request form on the
   tax office site), which states what was paid and when.
