# Sylphrena (Syl) — every description

Kaladin's spren: the honorspren bonded to him in The Stormlight Archive. Compiled
Saturday October 11, 2026 from "compile a document with every description you can find of
Kaladin's spren, and particularly what it looks like when she turns into a spear."

Lines in quotation marks are book text as quoted by the fan sources listed at the end. The
rest is those sources' composite description, drawn from across the five books.

## As a spear

The form she takes most often, because the spear is Kaladin's weapon.

- Glowing, silvery metal with glyphs engraved on the surface. As a spear the glyphs are
  swirling ones, along the sharpened sides of the spearhead.
- The grip is cylindrical and unadorned except for a helical swirl of metal about a foot
  long just below the tip. Where the spearhead meets the grip there is a single serration.
- Seen from outside, in Oathbringer: "a long silver Shardspear with a strange crossguard
  shape behind the tip."
- The length is not fixed. She comes long or short as he needs her: a short spear when he
  charges Amaram at Thaylen Field.

How the change itself looks:

- Blade to spear, Words of Radiance ch. 86, mid-duel with Szeth above the storm: "The
  weapon fuzzed to mist, then elongated and grew into the shape of a silvery spear, with
  glowing, swirling glyphs along the sharpened sides of the spearhead."
- Summoned straight to a spear, Rhythm of War ch. 2: "Syl formed into a majestic silvery
  spear as he swept his hand outward."
- The change runs through mist and takes no time at all. In the Szeth duel she goes spear,
  shield, halberd, hammer, knife and back again, each form arriving as he needs it.

## As a Blade, the sword form

- First summoning, Words of Radiance ch. 84, after he speaks the Third Ideal: "Wind blew in
  the opening in the wall, and Syl's ribbon of light became mist, a form she often took.
  Silver mist, which grew larger, coalesced before Kaladin, extending into his hand.
  Glowing, brilliant, a Shardblade emerged from the mist, vivid blue light shining from
  swirling patterns along its length."
- Sleek and beautiful, the Windrunner glyph centred on the blade, lines sweeping off it
  toward the hilt: grooves in the metal like flowing tresses of hair.
- A living Blade, so she arrives instantly instead of on the tenth heartbeat a dead Blade
  takes. Kaladin's own reaction: "Not ten heartbeats?" Let go or dismissed, she returns to
  mist.

## Her own form

- A young woman about a handspan tall, angular face, long flowing hair that fades to mist,
  a flowing white dress that fades the same way. Pale blues and whites. Face, hands and
  feet crisply distinct; the bust and hips of a slender woman. Barefoot.
- She glows like the afterimage of looking at the sun, but casts no light on anything.
- Size is hers to choose: from a ribbon small enough to twine in Kaladin's hair up to full
  human height, which she first took at the Tower in The Way of Kings ch. 67, watching the
  battle in horror. That was the shape she was in when she told him she was an honorspren
  and asked whether he knew the Words.
- More solid and more vivid in Shadesmar, and inside a highstorm.
- As the bond strengthened she began manifesting clothing in varying shades of blue.

## The other shapes she takes

- A long ribbon of blue-white light: her figure blurs into it when she springs into the
  air, twisting and warping like a strip of cloth caught in the wind.
- Mist, the form she passes through on the way to being a weapon, and one she often takes
  on its own.
- A swarm of glowing particles; a leaf; a small cloud; other non-human shapes when she
  would rather not be noticed.

## Spear-shaped, but not Syl

In Wind and Truth, Kaladin and Syl thrust their hands forward together and a spear of light
forms; when the light fades, the silvery spear standing there is made not of Syl but of
Honor, as the Honorblades are. An Honorspear rather than an Honorblade, a spear because it
is his.
