# Filing a DBA in Texas

Written September 26, 2026, from two notes three minutes apart: "Is the Texas SOS
direct open for me to file a DBA this weekend?" and "So I don't need SOS for a DBA?"
Texas calls it an **assumed name certificate**. Where it is filed depends entirely on
what the business already is.

**Decided Monday September 28, 2026: it is going to be an LLC.** The entity route below
is the one that applies. The county clerk is out.

## Where it goes

**Sole proprietorship or general partnership** (no registered entity): the **county
clerk** only, in the county of the principal office. Manor is in **Travis County**.
The Secretary of State is not involved at all.

- Travis County Clerk, Recording Division, (512) 854-9188
- $23.00 for one business name and one owner; $0.50 per additional owner on the same statement
- In the office: +$2.00 per acknowledgement. By mail: the form must be notarized first.
- Counter hours Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Closed weekends.
- Good for up to ten years, as stated on the application.

**LLC, corporation, LP, LLP** (a registered entity): the **Secretary of State** only,
Form 503, $25. Since House Bill 3609, effective September 1, 2019, registered entities
no longer file at the county as well, and the Travis County Clerk stopped recording
incorporated assumed names on that date.

## His filing

From "It's going to be an LLC so we're not going to do it at the clerk. We'll do it on
the SOS portal tomorrow.", spoken 5:36 a.m. Monday September 28, 2026.

**Tomorrow is Tuesday September 29** — the day SOSPortal opens at 8:00 a.m., and the
first day anything can be filed online after the outage. The plan lands on the earliest
date there was.

Two things to know before sitting down to it:

- **The LLC has to exist first.** "It's going to be" reads as an LLC not yet formed, and
  an assumed name certificate for an entity presumes the entity. Forming it is a
  different, larger filing: **Form 205, Certificate of Formation, $300** — plus the
  statutory 2.7% convenience fee if paid by card online, about $308, and $25 more for
  expedited processing. Also on SOSPortal.
- **Form 503 may not be needed at all.** The $25 assumed name certificate is only
  required when the LLC regularly does business under a name *other than* the legal name
  on its certificate of formation. If the name he wanted the DBA for is simply what the
  LLC will be called, Form 205 covers it and there is no Form 503 to file.

## The September 2026 outage

All Texas SOS online business filing went down at noon Friday September 25, 2026 and
returns **8:00 a.m. Tuesday September 29** as **SOSPortal**, which replaces SOSDirect
and SOSUpload. Nothing could be filed, searched or ordered online over that weekend.
The county clerk was not affected by the outage, only by being closed for the weekend:
the county route reopened Monday September 28, a day before the SOS route.

## Name search

Travis County will search its own records for name availability for $10.00. That is
county records only, not a statewide or trademark search. For an LLC the search that
matters is the SOS's own name availability check, which SOSPortal does as part of a
Form 205 filing: a certificate of formation is rejected if the name is not
distinguishable from an existing one.
