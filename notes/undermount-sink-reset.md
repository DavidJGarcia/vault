# Re-setting the undermount bathroom sink

The bathroom basin under the granite counter came loose. Asked about three times on the
evening of October 3, 2026: what a new basin costs, whether a new one skips the scraping,
and how to make the scraping go better.

## The job in short

An undermount basin is held to the underside of the stone by a continuous bead of 100%
silicone (plus clips or a rail on some sinks), not plumber's putty. Nothing bonds to cured
silicone, so the old bead has to come off the granite before anything goes back up — a new
sink does not avoid that half of the work. Support or clip the basin for 24 hours while the
new bead cures.

## Costs (prices read October 3, 2026)

- A basic white vitreous-china undermount basin: $50–100 at Home Depot or Lowe's; $130–250
  mid-range. A replacement must cover the cutout already in the stone.
- Doing it himself: a razor scraper and one tube of 100% silicone, about $8–12, plus an hour.
- A plumber instead: $230–650 typical sink labor, $230–800 for an undermount.

## Making the scraping go better

1. **Bulk first, with a fresh blade held almost flat.** Granite is harder than a razor, so
   a shallow angle will not scratch the stone. Change the blade when it starts to drag — a
   dull blade is most of why this job goes badly.
2. **Peel, don't chip.** Get one edge of the bead up, then pull a long ribbon off with
   fingers. Silicone comes away in ribbons and not in flakes.
3. **The thin film left behind is the real work, and a chemical does it.** Motsenbocker's
   Lift Off Silicone/Latex Caulk & Foam Sealant Remover: water-based, non-flammable, listed
   safe on granite, marble and stone. 16 oz for about $10 at Home Depot or Menards. Score
   the edges, spray, wait at least 3 minutes, agitate with a scraper, wipe with a clean
   white cloth, repeat. Do not let it sit more than 15 minutes. Heavy spots take two or
   three passes. It breaks the bond rather than dissolving the silicone.
4. **Not Goof Off on the stone** — it is xylene-based, flammable, and harsher than this
   needs to be.
5. **Last step before the new bead: wipe the granite underside with denatured alcohol and
   let it dry fully.** Silicone will not bond to dust, oil, or its own cured film. Bare,
   dry stone is the whole requirement; a polished look is not.
6. **It is overhead work.** Safety glasses, and a drop cloth in the cabinet: scrapings and
   spray come straight down. If the basin is already off, do its rim on a bench, and a
   plastic razor there rather than steel — china and stainless scratch where granite does
   not. A brand-new sink needs only the alcohol wipe.

## Two names that rhyme

Asked on the evening of October 3, 2026: "are you saying I can't use the liftoff spray on
the granite?" No — the opposite.

- **Lift Off** goes *on* the granite. Motsenbocker's Lift Off is water-based and labelled
  safe on granite, marble and stone. Its only limit is dwell time: do not leave it on the
  stone longer than 15 minutes.
- **Goof Off** stays off the granite. A different product, xylene-based, flammable, not
  listed for stone.
