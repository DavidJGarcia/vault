## Health

**Allergic to cefalexin.** Said Monday, September 21, 2026. The drug is cephalexin
(brand Keflex), a first-generation cephalosporin antibiotic — "cefalexin" is the same
drug, spelled the international way. The reaction and how severe it is were not said.

Worth saying out loud to any doctor, dentist or urgent care before an antibiotic is
prescribed for her.

The antibiotics closest to it, by shared side chain, are the ones most likely to cause
the same reaction: ampicillin and cefaclor share cephalexin's R1 side chain, and
amoxicillin and cefadroxil carry the closely related hydroxy version. Cephalosporins
with dissimilar side chains — cefuroxime, ceftriaxone — are the low-risk ones. That is
background for the conversation, not a substitute for it: her doctor decides what she
can take.
