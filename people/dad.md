# Dad

David's father. Written by the weekly pass of Saturday, October 4, 2026, because
everything known about him was scattered across records from one week and no document
held it — and he had already asked once, on Thursday, September 18, 2026, "What do you
know about my dad's situation?"

## The facts

- **Born Monday, May 11, 1955.** Said Thursday, September 17, 2026. He is 71, and turns
  72 on Tuesday, May 11, 2027. The date is now a line in `dates.md`, so the morning
  briefing will carry it.
- **Admitted to hospital in September 2026.** First mentioned Thursday, September 17 at
  6:18 am, with David there. His room was **North Tower, room 411**, said Friday,
  September 18 at 12:19 pm.
- **Blood glucose 125 mg/dL**, read out Thursday, September 17 at 6:18 am. David first
  said "That's glucose at 125" and two minutes later corrected it: the reading was his
  dad's, not his own.
- David ate hospital cafeteria meals on Friday, September 18 — breakfast and lunch — so
  he was there two days running.

## What has never been said

- **The reason for the admission, and any diagnosis.** Never stated in any capture.
- **The name of the hospital.** Never stated. Possibly in Temple, Texas: he asked why
  flags were at half-mast in Temple at 4:01 pm on Friday, September 18, the same day he
  ate twice in the hospital cafeteria. That is an inference from one coincidence and
  nothing more.
- **The admission date.** Nothing dad-related exists before 6:18 am on Thursday,
  September 17, and the Wednesday, September 16 records are an ordinary evening at home —
  a gym session and dinner — so the admission was most likely Wednesday night or early
  Thursday.
- **How it ended.** Nothing about his dad has been captured since Friday, September 18,
  2026. Whether he went home, is still in, or something else, David has not said. That
  silence is sixteen days old as of this writing, and it is the one thing this document
  most needs.
