# The Jensens

A family David knows. Started Sunday, October 11, 2026 from "Jensen's son is named
Edmund." and corrected by him in writing the same morning at 11:38 am: "I was trying
to say that the Jensen's son is named Emmet. There's no Edmund or Dickinson. Those
were mistranscriptions."

## The facts

- **The Jensen son is named Emmet.** Said Sunday, October 11, 2026 at 11:28 am, said
  again at 11:34 am, and spelled by him at 11:38 am. Emmet with one t is his own
  spelling, typed.

## What the ring got wrong

The one fact arrived twice and the name was garbled both times: "Edmund" at 11:28 am,
and "The Dickinson son is named Emmett." at 11:34 am, which read at the time as a
second, unrelated family. There is no Edmund and there are no Dickinsons. The second
record was deleted as the duplicate it turned out to be, and `people/dickinson.md` is
kept only as a pointer to this file.

## What has never been said

- **Who the Jensens are to him.** The name appears nowhere else in this vault and in
  no earlier record: not the six kids (David, Derek, Abigail, Jeffrey, Andrew, Cody),
  not on `people/catch-up.md` (Julie Cahoon, Dave Jenkins, Stacy Shablin), and not
  among the work names on file (Cody, Earl). A friend, a neighbour, a family at the
  church or the school are all open.
- **Who the parents are**, Emmet's age, and whether there are other children. "The
  Jensen son", with the article, reads as a household known by its surname and having
  one son.
- **Whether the new family has anything to do with them.** Four names came in between
  the two garbled ones: "Natalia, Arum, Torum, and Albium are the new family." at
  11:31 am (`people/new-family.md`). He corrected only Edmund and Dickinson, so that
  note stands as filed and separate.
- The spelling of Jensen. Still as the ring transcribed it: Jenson, Jansen and
  Janssen sound the same.
