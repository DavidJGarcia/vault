person document, because nothing yet says which name belongs to whom; when that

## What has never been said

- **Who they are to him.** "The new family" is the whole of it: new neighbours,
  a family new to the church or the school, in-laws, a family he met through the
  kids \u2014 all open.
- **Which is parent and which is child**, and whether the four are all of them.
- **Whether they are people at all.** Read as people because Natalia is a
  common given name said first, the way a family is introduced. A "new family"
  could as easily be four pets or four characters in a game, and the receipt
  said so.
- **The spellings.** Natalia could be Nataliya or Natalie; Arum could be Arun or
  Aaron; Torum could be Torin, Toran or Tarun; Albium could be Albin, Alvin or
  Albie. All four are as transcribed, and unverified.
- Whether this note and "Jensen's son is named Edmund." \u2014 said three minutes

  clock.
