# The Page family

Ed Page and his family. Opened Monday November 9, 2026 from the captain's log of
Sunday November 8, 2026: "Yesterday was state conference. I saw Ed Page and,
briefly, his family there. He has a cute baby girl that he's holding and
apparently is on leave from his new job at OpenAI or went there."

## Ed Page

- Seen at **stake conference on Sunday November 8, 2026** — David said "state
  conference", which is the ring mishearing *stake conference*, the twice-yearly
  Sunday meeting of all the wards in a stake. So the Pages are in David's stake,
  or were visiting it.
- **A baby girl**, young enough that Ed was carrying her.
- His family was there too, met only briefly. No names, no count of children.
- **Work: a new job at OpenAI.** What David had heard was one of two things and
  he said so — either Ed is *on leave* from that new job, or he has only just
  *gone* there. He did not know which.
- Nothing public was found: one search for an Ed Page at OpenAI on November 9,
  2026 turned up nothing about him. He is a private acquaintance, not a public
  figure, so nothing here comes from the web.

## Probably the "Pageer family" of November 8

On Sunday November 8, 2026 David said *"The Pageer family is going to have a
baptism and a baby blessing the second weekend in December"* — Pageer being the
name as the ring heard it, with no other trace of it anywhere. A day later he
named **Ed Page**, holding a baby girl.

- A **baby blessing needs a new baby**, and Ed has one in his arms.
- **Page and Pageer** are the same surname heard twice by the same ring.
- Both are church context: a stake conference, and a baptism paired with a
  blessing.

That is strong but not certain, so it is written as a reading rather than a
fact. If it holds, the December weekend is theirs: **Saturday December 12 to
Sunday December 13, 2026** — a baptism (ordinarily the Saturday) and the baby
girl's blessing (ordinarily in Sunday sacrament meeting). See
`people/pageer.md`, which this file supersedes, and the `dates.md` line.
