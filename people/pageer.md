# The Pageer family — see `people/page.md`

Superseded Monday November 9, 2026. "Pageer" was the ring's spelling of a
surname David said once, on Sunday November 8, 2026, in a note about a baptism
and a baby blessing the second weekend in December.

A day later he named **Ed Page**, seen at stake conference on Sunday November 8
holding a baby girl — and a baby blessing needs a new baby. So this is very
probably the **Page** family, and everything about them is now in
`people/page.md`, including what is certain and what is only read.

The earlier reasoning about the December weekend (Saturday December 12 to
Sunday December 13, 2026, the baptism ordinarily on the Saturday and the
blessing in Sunday sacrament meeting) is kept there. This file stays only as a
pointer, in case the name turns out not to be Page after all.
