- **Batteries on the plane** — spare lithium batteries and power banks go in the
  carry-on, never in checked baggage (FAA). Up to 100 Wh needs no approval; 101 to 160 Wh
  needs the airline's approval; over 160 Wh is not allowed. If the carry-on is gate-
  checked, take the batteries out and keep them in the cabin. Terminals taped or in
  sleeves so nothing shorts.

  gel-like — peanut butter, hummus, yogurt, jam — counts as a liquid: 3.4 oz or less in the

  keep it near the top.
